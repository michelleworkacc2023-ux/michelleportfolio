Brand campaigns, measured on consideration

01

Consumer Behaviour & Brand Consideration

Skip the Drama: Taxi is on Uber · integrated OOH + digital
Core question: How do you shift a deeply-held habit — street-hailing — and win consideration for booking through the app?
Consumer InsightAudience SegmentationBrand LiftOccasion Targeting
S
Business challenge

How could Uber Taxi shift consumers away from the habit of street-hailing and increase consideration for booking a taxi through the app?

T
Audience insight

Consideration is won at the moment of need. We focused on the specific occasions and locations where people decide how to get a ride.

A
Strategic approach
  1. Segmented audiences using telco signals (billing and browsing behaviour) to reach people with genuine ride-hailing intent.
  2. Balanced reach against video-view depth so the message built both awareness and consideration, not just impressions.
  3. Placed OOH at ride-hailing hotspots to meet consumers at the exact occasion of use.
R
Measurement & result
+1.1ptsConsideration lift
~+8ptsAd awareness lift
Measured via Brand Lift Study
  • Growth in first-time Uber Taxi rides and quarter-on-quarter brand familiarity
  • Increased share of online ride-hailing trips
  • Over NT$200M in earned media value
